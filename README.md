For running the program, see wayfarer/README.md

Match your personality to your dream destination! From popular tourist locations to hidden gems, take a short personality quiz that will guide you to your next travel spot.

Similar to travelroulette.app, our site provides even greater customisation features that empowers you to find the one perfect place that fits your documents, budget, interests, and most importantly, lifestyle. Our model allows you to really discover hidden festivals, music, culture, food, and much more, that we're confident will interest you and fit the quirks our personality test spotted.

Code was made with assistance and input from Anthropic's Claude AI chatbot.
